Best Places to Study in Iowa City

When you need serious focus, high productivity, and minimal noise, these locations across campus and downtown Iowa City offer the best environments for long study sessions.

1. University of Iowa Main Library (Learning Commons)

Location: 125 W Washington St | Vibe: High-Energy & Group Friendly

The Main Library Learning Commons is the premier study space for University of Iowa students. Featuring individual cubicles, reserveable group study rooms equipped with whiteboards, and a full-service cafe on the first floor, it accommodates all studying preferences. Floor 2 and above provide quiet and silent study floors for deep concentration.

2. Iowa City Public Library

Location: 123 S Linn St | Vibe: Peaceful & Community Oriented

Located right in the Ped Mall, the Iowa City Public Library offers spacious seating, natural sunlight through large window displays, and dedicated quiet reading rooms. It is ideal for students seeking free high-speed Wi-Fi and charging outlets away from main campus crowds.

3. Pomerantz Business Library (Pappajohn Business Building)

Location: 21 E Market St | Vibe: Professional & Focused

Situated on the second floor of C220 PBB, this library provides tailored facilities for business research, finance assignments, and small team projects. It features modern ergonomic chairs, private study rooms, and quick access to campus dining options.
