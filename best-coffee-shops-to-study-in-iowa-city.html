Best Coffee Shops to Study in Iowa City

If you perform at your best with a warm espresso drink, ambient music, and a lively atmosphere, check out these top-rated coffee shops around town.

1. Java House

Address: 211 E Washington St | Espresso Specialty: Specialty Lattes & Bakery

A classic Iowa City favorite, Java House offers a cozy, nostalgic atmosphere with ample seating, dim lighting, and steady background music. It is ideal for reading literature, typing essays, or meeting up for group discussion.

  • Abundant electrical outlets along booth walls
  • Comfortable armchairs and wide wooden tables

2. Cortado Coffee & Cafe

Address: 26 S Clinton St | Espresso Specialty: Mediterranean Coffee & Pastries

Located directly across from the Pentacrest, Cortado boasts a sleek modern interior with bright white lighting and outdoor seating during warmer months. It is ideal for morning study sessions paired with fresh savory bites.

  • Prime location right next to campus buildings
  • Excellent espresso options and healthy lunch items

3. Press Coffee

Address: 1120 N Dodge St | Espresso Specialty: Pour-Overs & Cold Brew

Located slightly north of downtown, Press Coffee offers a relaxed neighborhood vibe with rustic wooden decor and natural lighting. It is a peaceful escape from busy campus corridors.

  • Quiet ambient noise level for focused reading
  • Easier parking options compared to downtown spots
